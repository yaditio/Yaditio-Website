# RTWall - Cantilever

**RTWall - Cantilever** is a specialized engineering software designed for analyzing and designing reinforced concrete cantilever retaining walls.

## Key Features

- **Stability Analysis**: Automated check for overturning, sliding, and soil bearing capacity safety factors.
- **Structural Design**: Reinforcement design for Stem, Heel, and Toe slabs based on design standards.
- **Interactive UI**: Real-time diagramming of soil pressures, loading cases, and moment/shear envelopes.
- **Report Generation**: Export full calculation sheets for engineering submittals.
