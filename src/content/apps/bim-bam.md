## Overview

BIM-BAM focuses on streamlining digital building workflows, bridging programmatic engineering logic with 3D BIM models.

### Highlights
- Rapid structural component modeling.
- Automatic schedule and quantity takeoff generation.
- Open-source Python codebase for custom AEC scripting.
