## Overview

RTWall-Cantilever is a specialized application built to solve retaining wall design problems efficiently. It handles earth pressure theories (Rankine/Coulomb), stability ratios, and structural reinforcement calculations.

### Highlights
- Real-time safety factor evaluation for Overturning, Sliding, and Bearing.
- Clean visual graph representation of soil pressure distribution.
- Extensible Python codebase for custom geotechnical parameters.
