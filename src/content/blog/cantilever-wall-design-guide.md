Designing a cantilever retaining wall requires balancing lateral earth pressure against soil resistance and concrete stem design.

## Core Stability Checks

1. **Overturning Safety Factor**: Ratio of resisting moments to overturning moments about the toe.
2. **Sliding Safety Factor**: Resistance provided by base friction and key against active lateral force.
3. **Bearing Capacity**: Ensuring maximum soil pressure under the toe does not exceed allowable soil bearing capacity.
